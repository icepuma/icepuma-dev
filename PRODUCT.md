## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Stefan Ruzitschka's personal homepage presents his projects, professional background, tools, weekly open-source schedule, music channels, and social links. Visitors can read the content and open its external links. Projects lead the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep the existing copy, URLs, content collections, list order, section anchors, and single static Astro page. Preserve six projects, seven stack categories and 21 tools, seven calendar entries, eight music channels, four social links, the bio, and the footer. All music channels remain in one static list.

## Brand Commitments

The approved redesign takes its visual reference from Stand Alone Complex: a minimal interface, cyan control marks, plain section names, always-visible navigation, and precise button feedback. Use IBM Plex Sans with JetBrains Mono. Do not invent custom logos. Build and review the working page directly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support light, dark, and system themes, keyboard access, small screens, reduced motion, and readable content without JavaScript. Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use at least 44px control targets.
