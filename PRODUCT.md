## Platform

## Product Purpose

Stefan Ruzitschka's personal homepage presents his projects, professional background, tools, weekly open-source schedule, music channels, and social links. Visitors can read the content and open its external links. Projects lead the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep the existing copy, URLs, content collections, list order, section anchors, and single static Astro page. Preserve six projects, seven stack categories and 21 tools, seven calendar entries, eight music channels, four social links, the bio, and the footer. All music channels remain in one static list.

## Brand Commitments

The approved world is Stand Alone Complex: a minimal instrument panel where cold cyan means interaction, warm amber means recorded data, and the light and dark themes read as institutional daylight and the green-cast night city. Keep plain section names, always-visible navigation, and precise control feedback, with the register reticle marking the section being read. Use IBM Plex Sans Condensed for names, IBM Plex Sans for text, and JetBrains Mono for data. Do not invent custom logos or reproduce franchise art, marks, or on-screen text, and do not invent case numbers, timestamps, or other fictional data. Build and review the working page directly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support light, dark, and system themes, keyboard access, small screens, reduced motion, and readable content without JavaScript. Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use at least 44px control targets.
