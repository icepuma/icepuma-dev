## Product Purpose

Stefan Ruzitschka's personal homepage presents his projects, professional background, tools, weekly open-source schedule, music channels, and social links. Visitors can read the content and open its external links. Projects lead the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep the existing copy, URLs, content collections, list order, section anchors, and single static Astro page. Preserve six projects, seven stack categories and 21 tools, seven calendar entries, eight music channels, four social links, the bio, and the footer. All music channels remain in one static list.

## Brand Commitments

The approved world is Braun, in the tradition of Dieter Rams and Dietrich Lubs: warm grey paper, white and anthracite bodies, black print, one signal orange for now and for action, and one green pilot lamp for "on". The hero is a receiver whose tuning scale is the real weekly schedule, whose needle and display follow the Berlin clock, and whose hyper-realistic aluminium knob switches between Light, Auto, and Dark. Navigation is a row of push-button keys that latch on the current section; section headings pair the plain English name with a small German sub-label. Keep plain section names, always-visible navigation without numbers or sliding indicators, no separator lines, and physical control feedback. Use Archivo alone, as Braun used one grotesque. Do not reproduce the Braun logo, product names, model numbers, or other marks, do not invent readings, statuses, or other fictional data, and never play sound. Build and review the working page directly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support light, dark, and system themes, keyboard access, small screens, reduced motion, and readable content without JavaScript. Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use at least 44px control targets.
